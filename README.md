## 🇨🇳 中文

做 15 秒以内的单条音乐短片，例如说唱 MV、时尚走秀音乐视频、复古潮流拼贴风短片。先把范围卡死：明确要完整歌曲 MV、超过 15 秒或多段长 MV 的，说明边界后直接停止，不猜默认值。再一次性问齐风格处理、画面形态（真人实拍 / 真人 + 动漫融合 / 纯动漫）、人物来源与音乐内容。成片由一次视频生成完成，音乐、人声、歌词、音效与画面同步产出，配 8-10 个镜头和逐镜节奏剪辑，屏幕动态文字默认纯英文包装。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 范围门先行：只交付 15 秒以内单条短片，完整 MV / 超过 15 秒 / 多段长 MV 明确挡回
- 风格提醒门：Y2K、赛博、暗黑、clean 等外部风格可融合进复古拼贴，或按纯外部风格现场学习并说明稳定性更低
- 画面形态三选：真人实拍 / 写实时尚表演、真人 + 动漫风融合、纯动漫二次元
- 人物与音乐各自成选：上传人物参考（锁身份与造型）或 AI 原创先锋时尚人物；原创歌曲（同步生成编曲与人声）、纯器乐 BGM 或上传参考音乐
- 成片结构：8-10 个 Shot、4 个连贯空间区段、5 个动作族、2 个设计主导镜头
- 最终 Prompt 目标 5700-6500 字符：逐字展示给用户，再用同一字符串生成，禁止摘要版

## 🇬🇧 English

Makes a single music short of 15 seconds or less: a rap MV, a fashion-walk music video, a retro-collage clip. Scope is locked first, so an explicit full-song MV, anything over 15 seconds, or a multi-part long MV is refused rather than improvised. Style handling, visual form (live-action, live-action plus anime, or pure anime), character source and music content are then collected in one batched confirmation. The finished clip comes out of a single video generation with music, vocals, lyrics, sound effects and picture synced, structured across 8-10 shots with rhythm-locked cuts and English-only on-screen typography.

### ✨ What it does

- Scope gate first: only a single short under 15 seconds, with full-song and multi-part MVs explicitly refused
- Style gate: fold Y2K, cyber, dark or clean into the retro-collage template, or handle a pure outside style live
- Three visual forms: live-action performance, live-action plus anime fusion, or pure anime
- Character and music choices: an uploaded reference that locks identity, or an AI-designed character, plus an original song with vocals, instrumental BGM or uploaded audio
- Structural targets: 8-10 shots, 4 continuous space zones, 5 action families and 2 hero shots
- Final prompt aimed at 5,700-6,500 characters, shown verbatim and executed character-for-character, never a summary
